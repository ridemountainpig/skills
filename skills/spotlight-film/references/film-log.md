# Film log

One row per finished film, newest last. `scripts/plan-dice.mjs` reads the last row and redraws
any axis that repeats it; Gate 8 compares the family sequence with the last row. Copy this file
into the project root (`film-log.md`) and keep it across films; append the row that
`plan-dice.mjs` prints once the film is delivered, with the real family sequence.

Families (short codes): `shrink` opening shrink · `title` deep title plane · `slot` type slot · `bloom` bloom beat ·
`vfun` V funnel claim · `ivfun` inverted V · `burst` full-frame proof burst · `frieze` · `only` content only ·
`insert` full-frame insert · `lift` · `apex` apex claim · `travel` window travel / one-take · `black` blackout ·
`fig` type-only figure · `macro` macro pull-back · `hero` hero hold · `sig` signature moment · `brand` brand hold.
